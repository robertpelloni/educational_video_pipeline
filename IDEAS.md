# AGGRESSIVE IDEAS & STRATEGIC PIVOTS

1. **WebAssembly / Browser-Native Preview Engine**:
   Compile lightweight FFmpeg via Emscripten / WebAssembly directly into the browser to enable instantaneous live client-side preview rendering before dispatching heavy GPU jobs to Celery.

2. **Automated TikTok/Reels Virality Scoring**:
   Train a specialized reward model on cross-platform engagement metrics fetched by `analytics_engine.py` to automatically score draft scripts before synthesis, discarding hook ideas that don't pass an engagement threshold.

3. **Multi-Agent Script Debate Mode**:
   Have two LLM personas (e.g. "The Skeptic" and "The Expert") debate complex educational topics to produce engaging multi-voice dialog with alternating TTS voices.

4. **Self-Healing Rendering Pipeline**:
   Integrate automated fallback handlers when Stable Diffusion generation fails or times out, falling back to public domain image archives or animated vector charts.

5. **Direct Video Live-Streaming Pipeline**:
   Pipe continuous video compilation directly into an RTMP stream for 24/7 educational ambient radio / live YouTube study streams.
